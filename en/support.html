Frequently asked questions

How do I back up the game progress?
The app also saves the game progress as a file “spielstand.json”. You can find it in the Files app under “On My iPad” or “On My iPhone” in the folder “CookiWorld”. From there you can copy it, for example, to iCloud Drive. The file contains all three worlds, the stars and the recipes.

How do I rename or delete a world?
On the start screen, tap “Spielstände” (saved games). Each world has a pencil (rename) and a bin (delete). Both only work after a small sum, so that children don’t delete anything by accident.

How do I delete everything?
Open “Optionen → Für Eltern” (Options → For parents, after a small sum), then “Spielstand löschen” (delete game progress). All three worlds, the stars and the recipes are then deleted. This cannot be undone.

Does the game need the internet?
No. CookiWorld runs without the internet and sends nothing anywhere.

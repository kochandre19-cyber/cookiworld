Privacy

CookiWorld is a game for children. We do not want any data from children, nor from parents.

In short

  • CookiWorld collects no data.
  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no tracking.
  • No server: the game does not talk to anyone on the internet.
  • The game progress stays on the device – and in its backups, if you use any (e.g. iCloud).

The app

What we don’t do: We collect no data. There is no account and no sign-in. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game sends nothing to the internet and loads nothing from it.

Where the game progress is stored: The worlds you build, the stars and the settings are saved on the device so that you can keep playing. A copy is kept as a file in the Files app (folder “CookiWorld”) so that you can back up the game progress. Like all data on the device, it can end up in backups you have set up yourself, for example an iCloud backup or a backup on your computer. We can never see the game progress.

What Apple shares with us: If “Share With App Developers” is turned on on the device (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple sends us crash reports without names. They help us fix bugs. If you test the app in advance via TestFlight, Apple sees your name and email address as well as your feedback and crash reports and shows them to us; this only applies to invited testers, not to the app from the App Store.

Purchases: The app currently contains no purchases.

Deleting: The game progress can be deleted in the app under “Optionen → Für Eltern” (Options → For parents). When the app is deleted, the game progress on the device is gone too; copies in your own backups remain until you delete them.

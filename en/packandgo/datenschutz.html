The app

Where your data is stored: Pack & Go has no user account, no sign-in and no server of our own. Game progress, coins and settings are stored only locally on the device.

Ads: Pack & Go shows ads via Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited: rewarded videos, which you watch voluntarily, and interstitial ads. In doing so, Google processes, among other things, your IP address, information about the device and the app, and your interactions with the ads. More from Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent: In the EU, the app first asks for your consent via Google’s consent dialog (User Messaging Platform, UMP). Without consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. You can change your consent at any time in the app under “Settings → Privacy settings”.

Legal basis: For ads you have consented to, the legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); where information is stored on or read from your device for this purpose, it is also your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG, German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, as described above.

Transfer to the USA: Google may also transfer data to the USA and process it there. Google gives details in its Privacy Policy.

Advertising identifier: The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and we do not use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

Purchases: Coin packs and “Remove ads” are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment data.

Analytics: We currently use no analytics services.

What Apple shares with us: If “Share With App Developers” is turned on on the device (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple provides us with anonymous crash and usage data. It helps us fix bugs.

The app

Where your data is stored: Pack & Go has no user account, no sign-in and no server of our own. Game progress, coins and settings are stored only locally on the device.

Ads: Pack & Go shows ads via Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited: rewarded videos, which you watch voluntarily, and interstitial ads. The app requests non-personalised ads only. For this, Google receives data to deliver the ads, measure their reach and protect against fraud, including your IP address, information about the device and the app, and your interactions with the ads. More from Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent: Even non-personalised ads require information to be stored on or read from your device, for example to limit how often the same ad appears and for aggregated reporting. For this, in the EU the app first asks for your consent via Google’s consent dialog (User Messaging Platform, UMP). The consent dialog is Google’s standard dialog (IAB TCF) and lists general purposes, including personalised advertising. Regardless of your choices, Pack & Go requests non-personalised ads only. Without this consent, Google delivers limited ads at most, without features that need an identifier on the device. You can change your consent at any time in the app under “Einstellungen → Datenschutz-Einstellungen” (Settings → Privacy settings).

Legal basis: For ads you have consented to, the legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); where information is stored on or read from your device for this purpose, it is also your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG, German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, as described above.

Transfer to the USA: Google may also transfer data to the USA and process it there. Google gives details in its Privacy Policy.

Advertising identifier: The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

Purchases: Coin packs and “Werbung entfernen” (Remove ads) are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment data.

Deleting your progress: In the app: gear icon (Settings) → “Spielstand zurücksetzen” (Reset progress). Alternatively delete the app, all data is stored only on the device. Purchases stay with Apple and can be restored via “Käufe wiederherstellen” (Restore purchases).

Analytics: We use the following analytics services:

  • TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Augsburg): Usage statistics that help us improve the game. The app reports when a level is won, lost, quit or restarted, when a booster is used, an ad video is watched or a purchase is completed, each with details such as the level number. In addition, technical details such as app and iOS version and device type are sent, together with a device identifier that, according to TelemetryDeck, is made unrecognisable (hashed) before it is sent. The statistics start with the app. From build 28 onwards, you can turn them off in the app under “Einstellungen → Datenschutz & Hilfe → Nutzungsstatistik” (Settings → Privacy & help → Usage statistics); no more signals are sent after that. TelemetryDeck privacy policy

What Apple shares with us: If “Share With App Developers” is turned on on the device (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple provides us with anonymous crash and usage data. It helps us fix bugs.
